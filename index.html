奥奥相机隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月10日

本政策说明「奥奥相机」（下称「本应用」）如何处理信息。本应用是工作相机，用于拍摄并在本机整理照片。开发者：kw0ng。联系邮箱：hellosectest2026@outlook.com。

保存在这台手机上的信息

  • 相机：仅在你拍摄时使用，用于生成照片。照片可以印上拍摄时间。开启护理模式并载入档案后，也可以印上床位和姓名。
  • 照片：保存在本应用内，默认不会进入系统相册。
  • 护理档案：只有你打开护理模式并建档或扫码后才会产生。档案包含你输入的姓名、床位，以及腕带条码或二维码上的文字。照片可以归入某一份档案。这些内容只写在这台手机的应用存储里。
  • 运动传感器：仅在使用本应用期间读取手机朝向，用于摆放水印和显示水平仪。读数只在设备上处理，不会单独保存或发送。
  • 设置：语言、水印开关、水印大小等偏好保存在这台手机上。

我们不收集的信息

  • 当前版本不获取地理位置，不申请定位权限，水印不含地点，相册也不按地点浏览。
  • 没有账号系统，不收集电话号码、电子邮箱或广告标识。
  • 照片、姓名、床位和腕带内容不会上传到开发者的服务器。开发者无法看到这些内容。
  • 不出售个人信息。应用内不嵌入第三方广告或统计工具。

如果你的手机里还有更早版本留下的地点文字，那些内容也只留在这台手机上。当前版本不会再新增地点信息。

分享

只有你主动点击分享时，才会通过系统分享面板把照片交给其他应用。若你在分享面板中选择存储到相册，照片才会写入系统相册。分享出去的照片里，已经印上的时间和档案文字会随照片一起交给你选择的应用。

保存与删除

  • 在应用内删除照片后，该照片即从本应用中移除。
  • 删除一份档案时，你可以选择只解除照片与档案的关联，或同时删除这些照片。
  • 关闭护理模式会清除这台手机上的全部档案。照片仍留在本应用的「全部」中，已印在照片上的文字不会被擦掉。
  • 卸载本应用会删除应用内的全部照片和档案。

权限

相机权限可以在系统「设置 → 奥奥相机」中随时关闭。当前版本不使用定位权限。写入系统相册的权限只在你分享并选择保存时才会用到。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，将替换本页面内容并修改生效日期。

Aoao Camera Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Aoao Camera handles information. It is a work camera for taking photos and organizing them on your phone. Developer: kw0ng. Contact: hellosectest2026@outlook.com.

Information stored on this phone

  • Camera: used only while you take a photo. Photos can be stamped with the time. When nursing mode is on and a chart is loaded, the stamp can also include the bed and name.
  • Photos stay inside the app. They are not added to the system photo library unless you choose to save them.
  • Charts exist only if you turn on nursing mode and create or scan a chart. A chart can contain the name and bed you type, and the text read from a wristband barcode or QR code. Photos can be filed under a chart. This stays in the app’s storage on this phone.
  • Motion: while the app is in use, the phone’s orientation is read to place the watermark and to show the level. Those readings are processed on the device and are not stored or sent on their own.
  • Preferences such as language and watermark settings stay on this phone.

Information we do not collect

  • This version does not access location, does not ask for location permission, does not put a place on the watermark, and does not group photos by place.
  • There is no account. We do not collect phone numbers, email addresses, or advertising identifiers.
  • Photos, names, beds, and wristband text are not uploaded to the developer. The developer cannot see them.
  • We do not sell personal information. The app has no third-party ads or analytics.

If an older version left place text on this phone, that text also stays on the device. This version does not add new place information.

Sharing

A photo leaves the app only when you tap Share and hand it to another app. It is written to the system photo library only if you choose Save in that share sheet. Time and chart text already printed on the photo go with the photo.

Keeping and deleting

  • Deleting a photo in the app removes it from the app.
  • When you delete a chart, you can unlink its photos or delete those photos as well.
  • Turning nursing mode off deletes every chart on this phone. Photos remain in All. Text already printed on a photo is not erased.
  • Deleting the app deletes all photos and charts stored in it.

Permissions

You can turn off camera access in Settings → Aoao Camera. This version does not use location access. Photo library access is used only when you share a photo and choose to save it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be replaced and the effective date will be updated.
